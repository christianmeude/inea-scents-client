# Product

## Platform

adaptive

## Users
- **Customers**: End users seeking to book a personalized perfume bar experience.

## Product Purpose
A perfume bar service reservation app. Success means customers can easily discover offerings, understand the brand's elegance, and seamlessly book their dates and packages with absolute clarity and zero friction.

## Positioning
Offers quality products and excellent service. It exudes elegance and grace, while being generous with the packages and services provided. The app should feel tactile, responsive, and deeply personal.

## Operating Context
Customers use this native Flutter mobile app on personal devices (iOS/Android). The app must guide them smoothly from discovery to booking confirmation. It communicates with the shared `inea-scents` backend.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Built in Flutter. Must provide an extremely polished, smooth mobile experience. Offline states, loading states, and form validations must be handled gracefully to prevent any customer anxiety during the booking process.

## Brand Commitments
- Core aesthetic values: Elegance and grace
- The experience must feel like a "Concierge" – helpful, anticipating needs, and flawless.
- Existing Facebook business page: https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61580331093927

## Product Principles
- **Frictionless Booking**: Every step from date selection to payment must feel effortless and explicitly clear.
- **Visual Warmth**: The UI must embody the brand's warm, floral elegance. Hard edges and harsh colors are strictly forbidden.
- **Tactile Feedback**: Every interaction (tapping a package, picking a date) must have satisfying, soft feedback.
